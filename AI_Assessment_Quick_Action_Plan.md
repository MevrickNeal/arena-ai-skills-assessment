# ⚡ EMERGENCY ACTION PLAN
### AI Skills Assessment — Same-Day Execution Guide

---

> **Situation:** The deadline is TODAY (1st November 2026). Here's how to get this done FAST.

---

## Option A: Quick Turnaround (4-6 Hours)

### Step 1: Send the Questionnaire Digitally (30 minutes)
- Convert the questionnaire to a **Google Form** or **Microsoft Form**
- Email it to ALL developers with this message:

---

> **Subject:** URGENT: AI Skills Assessment — Please Complete by [Time Today]
>
> Dear Team,
>
> As directed by the Managing Director, we are conducting a company-wide assessment of AI skills and expertise across our development team.
>
> Please complete this short questionnaire (10-15 minutes):
> **[Insert Form Link]**
>
> ⏰ **Deadline: [Time] today, 1st November 2026**
>
> Your honest responses will help shape the company's AI strategy and identify training opportunities.
>
> Thank you for your prompt cooperation.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your Name]
> Project Coordinator

---

### Step 2: Follow Up (Ongoing)
- Send a reminder after 2 hours
- Call/message developers who haven't responded
- Aim for at least 70% response rate

### Step 3: Compile Results (1-2 hours)
- Export form responses to a spreadsheet
- Calculate averages and fill in the report template
- Identify top skills and gaps

### Step 4: Complete the Report (1-2 hours)
- Fill in the report template with actual data
- Write the Executive Summary last (after you have all data)
- Focus on Sections 1, 4, 7, and 9 — these are what the MD cares about most

---

## Option B: If You Can't Reach All Developers Today

### Present a Partial Report + Plan

Tell the MD:
1. ✅ Here is the **assessment framework** I've designed (show the questionnaire)
2. ✅ Here are the **results so far** from [X] developers who responded
3. ✅ Here is the **report format** with preliminary findings
4. 📋 Here is my **timeline** to complete the remaining interviews by [proposed date]

> **This is actually a professional approach** — it shows you have a structured methodology even if full data collection is ongoing.

If short on time, focus on these sections of the report:

| Priority | Section | Why |
|:---:|---------|-----|
| 🔴 1 | Executive Summary | MD reads this first (and possibly only this) |
| 🔴 2 | Summary of Findings (Section 4) | Hard numbers and data |
| 🔴 3 | Skills Gap Analysis (Section 7) | Actionable intelligence |

## Quick Google Form Setup Guide

### Questions to Include (Minimum Viable Survey):

1. **Full Name** (Short answer)
2. **Department** (Dropdown)
3. **Job Title** (Short answer)
4. **Rate your overall AI/ML knowledge** (Linear scale 1-5)
5. **Which AI tools/frameworks have you used?** (Checkboxes: Python ML, TensorFlow, PyTorch, OpenAI API, Hugging Face, LangChain, Prompt Engineering, None, Other)
6. **Have you worked on AI/ML projects?** (Yes/No)
7. **If yes, briefly describe your most significant AI project** (Paragraph)
8. **Do you use AI tools in your daily work?** (Yes/No)
9. **If yes, which ones?** (Checkboxes: GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, Gemini, Other)
10. **What AI training would you like?** (Paragraph)
11. **Rate your readiness to contribute to an AI project** (Linear scale 1-5)

> This takes ~5 minutes to fill out and gives you enough data for the report.
