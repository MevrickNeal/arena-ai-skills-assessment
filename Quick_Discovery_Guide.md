# Quick Discovery Script — Finding Your 15 Developers
### Who Are They? What Are They Working On?

---

> ⏰ **You don't have time to figure this out slowly. Here's exactly how to get this info in 30 minutes.**

---

## METHOD 1: Ask One Key Person (Fastest — 10 minutes)

**Who to call/message RIGHT NOW:**

| Person to Ask | What They Know | Ask This |
|---------------|----------------|----------|
| **HR Manager / HR Executive** | Full staff list, designations, departments | "Can you share the list of all developers with their departments and contact details?" |
| **CTO / Tech Lead / Senior Dev** | Who's on what project, tech stack used | "Can you give me a quick rundown of all active projects and which devs are on each?" |
| **Your direct manager** | Team structure, project assignments | "I need the developer roster for the MD's AI assessment — who do I contact?" |
| **Office admin / IT** | Email distribution lists | "Is there a developer mailing list or Teams/Slack channel?" |

### Sample Message to Send:

> **WhatsApp / Slack / Teams message:**
>
> "Hi [Name], hope you're doing well. The Managing Director has asked me to conduct an AI skills assessment across all our developers. Could you please share:
> 1. The list of all developers (names + contact info)
> 2. Which projects each developer is currently assigned to
>
> I need this urgently — thanks a lot! 🙏"

---

## METHOD 2: Check These Internal Sources (15 minutes)

| Source | How to Access | What You'll Find |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Company email directory** | Outlook → Address Book / Google Workspace → Directory | All employee names & emails |
| **Slack / Microsoft Teams** | Check #development or #engineering channels | Active developers, what they're discussing |
| **Jira / Trello / Asana** | Project boards → Team members | Who's assigned to what project |
| **GitHub / GitLab / Bitbucket** | Organization → Members / Recent commits | Active developers and their repositories |
| **Attendance / HRIS system** | HR portal | Full employee roster with departments |
| **Previous project reports** | Your files / shared drives | Past project assignments |

---

## METHOD 3: Group Broadcast (If You Can't Find a List)

> **Post this in the company's general developer channel (Slack/Teams/WhatsApp group):**

---

> **📢 Attention All Developers**
>
> Hi everyone! As directed by the Managing Director, I'm conducting a quick AI Skills Assessment for the company.
>
> If you are a developer, please reply to this message with:
> 1. Your full name
> 2. Your department/team
> 3. The project(s) you're currently working on
>
> I'll then send you a short questionnaire (takes only 5-10 minutes).
>
> Thanks!
> — [Your Name], Project Coordinator

---

---

## YOUR 30-MINUTE PLAYBOOK

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| **0-5 min** | Message HR or your manager for the developer list |
| **5-15 min** | While waiting, check Slack/Teams/Jira/GitHub for developer names |
| **15-20 min** | Start filling in the Developer Registry with whatever you have |
| **20-25 min** | Create the Google Form (use the short 11-question version from the Action Plan) |
| **25-30 min** | Send the survey to everyone you've identified so far |
| **Ongoing** | Add more developers as you discover them; send them the survey too |

---

## KEY INSIGHT

> **You said they're working "individually" — this is actually EASIER.**
>
> It means you don't need to navigate complex team hierarchies. Each developer likely:
> - Has their own project or is the sole dev on a project
> - Can speak directly about their own skills without needing team lead approval
> - Can fill out the questionnaire independently
>
> **Just find their names and contacts, send the survey, and let them self-report.**
